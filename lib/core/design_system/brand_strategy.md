# Babka Brand Strategy & Visual Identity (v1.0.0)

## Brand Values
- **Authenticity**: Honoring traditional Persian baking methods.
- **Craftsmanship**: Every bread is a work of art.
- **Warmth**: The comfort of a local bakery, elevated to a premium level.
- **Simplicity**: An effortless, calm ordering experience.

## Design Language: "Artisanal Precision"
- **Warm, never cold**: Using soft creams and golden tones.
- **Premium, never luxurious for the sake of luxury**: Quality materials and spacing.
- **Handmade, never rustic or rough**: Clean execution with organic touches.
- **Modern, never futuristic**: Respecting tradition while being clearly built for today.
- **Calm, never busy**: Every screen should be a moment of peace.

## Photography Guidelines (The Visual North Star)
Product photography is the most important visual asset.
- **Lighting**: Warm, natural "golden hour" lighting.
- **Focus**: Shallow depth of field to highlight texture and crust.
- **Framing**: Consistent, premium framing (Bread as the hero).
- **Context**: Minimal, natural backgrounds (stone, wood, flour).

## Component Philosophy: The Signature Product Card
- The `ProductCard` is the brand's signature.
- **Immutable Rules**: Fixed image ratios, padding, and badge placements.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Clear hierarchy where photography is the focal point.

## Motion & Interactivity
- Animations must feel smooth, calm, and intentional.
- Durations: 120ms (taps), 180ms (cards), 300ms (page transitions).
- Microinteractions: Morphing buttons (Add -> Quantity) and elegant fills.
